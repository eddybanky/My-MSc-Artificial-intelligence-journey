# MSc Artificial Intelligence – University of Essex Online
This e-portfolio documents my learning journey throughout the MSc Artificial Intelligence programme at the University of Essex Online. It contains my modules, units, assignments, projects, research notes, and professional development. Each section is organised to clearly demonstrate progression, technical skills, and academic achievement.

## Structure
- **Modules** – All taught modules, broken down into units with notes, assignments, and practical work.
- **Projects** – Independent and module-linked AI projects.
- **Research** – Literature reviews, experiment logs, and academic investigations.
- **CV** – Professional CV and supporting documents.

This portfolio reflects my commitment to structured learning, continuous improvement, and applied artificial intelligence practice.
